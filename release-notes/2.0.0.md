### New
- **The face shows what Claude Code is doing.** It twinkles while Claude works, looks up when it needs you and smiles when it's done. Connect it in Settings → Face & Mirror.
- **AirPods show each earbud and the case.** Left, right and case, with charging, when you connect them.
- **Join video calls from the notch.** Calendar events get a Join button, and the notch gives you a heads-up two minutes before a call.
- **Find screenshots by the words in them.** Clipboard search reads copied images on your Mac, and right-click offers Copy Text. Snippets keep text you paste often.
- **Make it yours.** Swipe on the music to skip, lyrics under the closed notch, an output menu, calmer or livelier motion, and apps where hovering doesn't open the notch.

### Improved
- **Lighter than ever.** About 0.1% CPU in the background and around 30 MB, measured on every release; a scrolling title with the notch open dropped from 18% CPU to under 1%.
- **Settings are grouped by feature.** Music, Face & Mirror and Volume & Brightness each have their own place.
