### New
- **Runs on Intel Macs too.** NotchFun now works on the Intel Macs that run macOS Tahoe, not only on Apple Silicon.
- **See how long Caffeine has left.** A timed session counts down beside the cup; switch it off in Settings → Caffeine if you'd rather not.
- **Updates itself.** New versions download quietly and install the next time you quit. You can turn this off in Settings → About.

### Improved
- **A friendlier setup.** Clearer next steps on every screen, a hello from the notch, and Launch at login switched on for you.
- **A lighter, livelier notch.** Slimmer icons, a little bounce when the volume popup appears, and play and pause animate.

### Fixed
- **Slow drags open the shelf.** Dragging a file up gently on a trackpad sometimes didn't open the notch; now it always does.
- **The mirror opens on the first click.** It sometimes needed two, and could be slow to respond.
