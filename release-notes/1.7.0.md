### Improved
- **NotchFun stays light however long your Mac stays on.** Each time you locked your screen, an invisible copy of the notch kept running in the background. After a few days that added up to noticeable CPU, battery and memory use. Now it uses almost none, the same on day five as at launch.
- **The face doesn't blink while your display is asleep.** Nobody is watching, so it waits.
