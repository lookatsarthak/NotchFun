### New
- **Forget old clips automatically.** Settings → Clipboard → "Forget clips unused for" removes clips you haven't copied or pasted in a while. Pinned clips are always kept.
- **Nothing changes for you until you choose.** If you already use clipboard history, it keeps everything as before; new installs start at one week.

### Fixed
- **Setup remembers your volume popup choice.** Quitting partway through setup and coming back no longer forgets it.
