### Improved
- **Cleaner quitting.** NotchFun now shuts down properly at log out and during updates, so nothing it was about to save is lost.
- **Smoother notch.** Hovering, dragging and pressing volume keys redraw far less of the open notch.
- **Settings apply instantly.** A changed setting shows in the notch straight away.
- **Keyboard shortcuts work better on macOS 27.**

### Fixed
- **The Now Playing helper no longer keeps running** after NotchFun quits.
