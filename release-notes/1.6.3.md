### Fixed
- **Now Playing stays on after a restart.** If the Mac was still starting up when NotchFun checked whether Now Playing works, it could wrongly decide it didn't, switch it off and ask you to choose a music app on every launch. It now checks again before believing that, and this update re-checks for everyone.

### Improved
- **Opening NotchFun again shows Settings.** From Applications, Spotlight or Launchpad, while it's already running.
- **The smiley face costs less.** It blinks with a quick cut instead of an animation, which takes a fraction of the energy.
- **New setups start with recommendations.** Now playing, the shelf, clipboard history, the volume and brightness popup and the smiley face start on; Caffeine and the menu bar icon start off. Your settings don't change.
- **Setup says what's waiting on Accessibility.** If you skip it, the last screen says what won't work yet and lets you allow it there.
