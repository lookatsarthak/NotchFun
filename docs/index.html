    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No trial, no account, no in-app purchases. It's open source under the GPLv3.

    Why does macOS warn me when I first open it?

    Apple only skips that warning for apps notarised through its Developer Program, which costs US$99 a year. NotchFun is a free side project, so it isn't notarised yet. It isn't a finding about the app.

    Install with the Terminal line above and you won't see it at all. Otherwise it's a one-time step: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    Any Mac on macOS 26 Tahoe or later — Apple Silicon, or an Intel Mac that runs Tahoe. On macOS 15 Sequoia, NotchFun 1.2.1 still works.

    What permissions does it need?

    Only what the features you turn on need: Calendar for events, Camera for the mirror, Accessibility for pasting and the volume popup. Setup asks at the moment it matters.

    Does anything leave my Mac?

    The app has no accounts and no analytics. It checks GitHub for updates, and if you turn on lyrics it looks up the current song on LRCLIB. Your clipboard never leaves your Mac. This website and the installer keep anonymous counts of visits, clicks and installs — no cookies, nothing that identifies you.
