    The notch, but fun.

    NotchFun turns the MacBook notch into a small, quiet place for what's playing, files you're moving around, what you've copied, and more.

    Download for Mac View on GitHub

    Latest release · Apple Silicon and Tahoe-capable Intel Macs

    See it work ↓

    01 — Now playing

    What's playing,right where you look.

    Artwork, controls and a live waveform sit around the notch while music plays. Open it for the scrubber, shuffle and repeat. Apple Music, Spotify, and anything else that reports Now Playing.

    Real life: skip a track mid-call without hunting for the app. Close the notch and the artwork stays, tucked either side of it.

    ↑ Watch the notch

    Press play, then keep scrolling: the notch keeps the music with you.

    02 — Shelf

    A pocket forfiles in motion.

    Drag anything toward the notch and it opens into a shelf. Drop it, carry on, and pick it up later — or AirDrop and share straight from there.

    Real life: collect screenshots from three apps, then drag all of them into one email.

    Everything you copied,a glance away.

    Text, links, images and files, with the app each came from. Start typing to search, pin the ones you reuse, and paste straight back into whatever you were doing. It never records passwords, and it stays on your Mac.

    Real life: copy a tracking number in the morning, paste it into a support chat at four.

    macOS says it “could not verify” NotchFun?

    NotchFun isn't notarised by Apple — that needs a paid developer account — so macOS shows this for the first open. It isn't a finding about the app.

    1. Click Done on the warning (not Move to Bin).
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    3. Next to “NotchFun was blocked”, click Open Anyway.

    Only once. The Terminal install skips this entirely.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. NotchFun is open source under the GPLv3. No trial, no account, no in-app purchases.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    Any Mac on macOS 26 Tahoe or later: Apple Silicon, or one of the four Intel Macs that run Tahoe. 1.2.1 remains available for macOS 15 Sequoia.

    What permissions does it need?

    Only the ones for features you switch on — Calendar for events, Camera for the mirror, Accessibility for pasting from history and for the volume and brightness popup. Setup asks for each at the moment it matters.

    Does anything leave my Mac?

    There are no accounts and no analytics. It checks GitHub for updates, and if you show lyrics it looks up the current song on LRCLIB. Clipboard history never leaves your Mac.

    How is it related to boring.notch?

    NotchFun is a fork of boring.notch by TheBoredTeam, who built the foundation. This fork adds clipboard history, AirPods battery, Shortcuts actions, a new setup, and a lot of fixes and polish.
